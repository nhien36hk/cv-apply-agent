# CV Auto-Apply Plugin Rules

When the user asks to tailor CVs, discover jobs, or pre-fill job applications:
1. Read and follow `skills/cv-auto-apply/SKILL.md` strictly phase-by-phase without skipping any Phase Exit Gate.
2. Never fabricate metrics, tools, education details, or JD-only keywords in `cv.md`, tailored CVs, or Cover Letters.
3. Never bypass CAPTCHA / Cloudflare Turnstile or click final Submit automatically; always stop at the confirmation button for human submission.
